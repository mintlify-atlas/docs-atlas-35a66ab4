## What WhatCable tells you

Every USB-C cable looks identical from the outside, but carries wildly different capabilities — from USB 2.0 charge-only to 240 W / 80 Gbps Thunderbolt 4. WhatCable reads three families of IOKit services on Apple Silicon Macs to give you a complete picture per port.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install WhatCable">
    Download from the [Releases page](https://github.com/darrylmorley/whatcable/releases/latest) or run `brew install --cask whatcable`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the menu bar icon">
    A popover opens showing every USB-C port on your Mac and what is currently plugged in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plug in a cable">
    The port card updates in real time, showing cable speed, power capacity, and a charging diagnostic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the CLI (optional)">
    Run `whatcable --json | jq` from your terminal for structured output you can pipe into scripts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
WhatCable requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later and an Apple Silicon Mac. Intel Macs are not supported because the Thunderbolt 3 controller on Intel hardware does not expose USB-PD negotiation state through any public IOKit accessor.
</Note>
